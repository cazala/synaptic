Real BPTT

Each update samples a 64–96 generation horizon, then scores its last sixteen states and differentiates through the whole trajectory. Consecutive targets turn the selected image into an attractor instead of one transient frame.

Regeneration curriculum

Evolved states return to their slots in a persistent sample pool. The worst sample is reseeded, while healthy samples are later damaged to widen the target's basin of attraction.
