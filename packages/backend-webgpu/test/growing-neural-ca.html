Real BPTT

Each update samples a 64–96 generation horizon, then scores its last sixteen states and differentiates through the whole trajectory. Consecutive targets turn the flower into an attractor instead of one transient frame.

Regeneration curriculum

Evolved states return to their slots in a persistent sample pool. The worst sample is reseeded, while healthy samples are later damaged to widen the target's basin of attraction.

Portable result

The four learned tensors export as the versioned Growing Neural CA JSON artifact understood directly by @cazala/automata.
